# Questions for the Business Owner & Chartered Accountant (CA)

Status: Initialized for Phase 0 tracking.

## A. Tax and Legal
- **A1:** Is the business GST-registered? GSTIN? Regular scheme or composition scheme? (Default: Regular)
- **A2:** Which GST rates apply to yarn, fabric, freight, and job-work, and does the CA confirm them? (Default: Yarn 5%, Fabric 5%)
- **A3:** Are HSN codes required on invoices (and how many digits)? (Default: 4 to 8 digits)
- **A4:** Are e-invoicing or e-way bills applicable now or in the near future? (Default: Future release)
- **A5:** Are TDS or TCS applicable? (Default: Future release)
- **A6:** Who is the CA, and will they review the chart of accounts and sample reports?

## B. Documents & Invoicing
- **B1:** Collect sample of the current sales invoice format.
- **B2:** Preferred invoice number format and prefix. (Default: `INV/26-27/0001`)
- **B3:** Invoice text: terms and conditions, bank account details for payment, declaration, logo.
- **B4:** Are prices quoted and entered tax-exclusive? (Default: Yes)
- **B5:** Is the invoice total rounded to the nearest rupee? (Default: Yes)
- **B6:** Are transport, packing, or freight charges billed as separate line items?

## C. Opening Balances & Cut-off
- **C1:** Agreed go-live cut-over date.
- **C2:** Are old unpaid bills tracked bill-by-bill or as a single lump-sum balance per party? (Default: Bill-by-bill)
- **C3:** Opening cash in hand and bank account balances at cut-over date.
- **C4:** Existing loans, machinery/fixed assets, or other liabilities to record as opening balances.
- **C5:** Can the previous system or spreadsheet export clean opening data?

## D. Products and Units
- **D1:** Units used for yarn (kg, bags, cones) and fabric (metres, rolls, pieces). (Default: kg for yarn, metres for fabric)
- **D2:** Is the same fabric ever sold in more than one unit?
- **D3:** Typical product catalog and default selling/purchase rates.

## E. Money Flows & Banking
- **E1:** Payment modes used (Cash, UPI, NEFT/RTGS, Cheque).
- **E2:** Are customer payments matched to specific bills or received "on account"? (Default: Bill-by-bill allocation with advance support)
- **E3:** Are early-payment cash discounts offered? (Default: No)
- **E4:** How are worker wages paid (daily, weekly, monthly; cash or bank)? (Default: Expense on payment)
- **E5:** Are operational expenses ever purchased on credit? (Default: Expenses recorded when paid)

## F. Business Model & Operations
- **F1:** Does the business perform weaving for others on a job-work basis? (Default: No, manufactures own fabric)
- **F2:** How frequent are returns, weight shortages, quality rejections, or rate difference claims?
- **F3:** Does the business sell to unregistered consumers or out-of-state buyers?
- **F4:** UI and invoice language preference (English only or bilingual Tamil/English)? (Default: English)
